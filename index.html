How we build

Axiom Zeta builds operator-owned AI for people who refuse to rent their own judgment. Three principles survive contact with every roadmap:

Sovereign by default

You own the system. Local-first where it matters, exportable everywhere, and never designed to hold your data hostage. If Axiom Zeta disappeared tomorrow, your work should survive intact.

Signal over noise

Less, but decisive. One brief a week, not 400 alerts. Every product is edited for the decision, not the feed.

Honest by design

Labeled, sourced, verifiable. Simulated is labeled simulated. Pricing is plain. We would rather under-promise in writing than over-promise in marketing.

Built in the open

The studio ships in public. Live products, public pricing, and cancel-anytime terms — the work should speak before the marketing does.
