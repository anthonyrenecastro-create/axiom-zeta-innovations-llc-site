How we build

E.L.L.I.A. builds operator-owned AI for people who refuse to rent their own judgment. Three principles survive contact with every roadmap:

Sovereign by default

You own the system. Local-first where it matters, exportable everywhere, and never designed to hold your data hostage. If E.L.L.I.A. disappeared tomorrow, your work should survive intact.

Signal over noise

Less, but decisive. One brief a week, not 400 alerts. Every product is edited for the decision, not the feed.

Honest by design

Labeled, sourced, verifiable. Simulated is labeled simulated. Pricing is plain. We would rather under-promise in writing than over-promise in marketing.

Open source, non-profit

Every E.L.L.I.A. product is public source — read it, audit it, fork it, self-host it. The software is free under an open-source license. We're a non-profit, sustained by donations, grants, and community support. Contributions keep the lab running; they never pay for the code itself. Browse the source.
